# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Facility managers, operations directors, business owners, and property managers across Toronto and the GTA who are choosing — or replacing — a commercial cleaning vendor. They are evaluating trust and standards more than price: they need to believe the work will be consistent, discreet, and genuinely thorough before they hand over keys to their space. Most arrive from search or referral, on desktop during the workday or on mobile between meetings, ready to request a quote if the site earns it quickly.

## Product Purpose

A single conversion-focused landing page for Lumeco, a premium commercial cleaning company. Its job is to make a high-standard first impression and turn a qualified visitor into an estimate request. Success is a completed "free estimate" form (or a call), from a visitor who now believes Lumeco operates at a higher standard than the typical cleaning vendor.

## Positioning

Lumeco is the premium commercial cleaning company for people who refuse to settle for "clean enough" — refined spaces, guaranteed standards, every visit. The single claim every section reinforces: *this is the higher-standard choice.*

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: **Get a free estimate** (the form lives in the hero, first fold).
- Secondary CTA: **Call us** — for visitors who want a person, not a form.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: *Spaces refined. Standards unmatched.*
- Belief ladder (in order): (1) these people are serious professionals, not a gig crew; (2) they've done this at scale for a long time — 30+ years, 500+ facilities; (3) they'll tailor the plan to my space and budget; (4) the quality is guaranteed, insured, and low-risk to try; (5) requesting an estimate is easy and commits me to nothing.
- Proof on hand: 30+ years in business, 500+ facilities served, 98% retention, strong review rating, insured/bonded/guaranteed. Real client testimonials and logos to be supplied by the owner (placeholders in place until then).

## Brand Personality

Premium, refined, meticulous. The voice is quietly confident — it states standards rather than shouting deals, and it never begs. It should evoke confidence and calm: the feeling of walking into a space that has been cared for by someone who takes pride in it. Restraint is the register; the brand shows the standard rather than describing it.

## Anti-references

Do **not** look like the modal commercial-cleaning site: a blue-to-green gradient washed over a stock photo of two staff, a bright generic-corporate-blue palette, and a wall of identical icon-heading-text cards. Do not look AI-generated: uniform evenly-spaced sections, centered-everything, timid gray-on-white restraint, an eyebrow kicker above every heading, or the SaaS hero-metric template. If a visitor could say "a template made this," it has failed.

## Design Principles

Refined restraint — commit to a navy-drenched world and let the teal spark do the work of ten decorations; empty space is a feature, not a gap. Show the standard — art-direct real, pristine imagery and precise detailing instead of describing quality in adjectives. Quiet confidence — one clear primary action per fold, no shouting, no urgency gimmicks. Proof over hype — years, facilities, retention, and guarantees carry more weight than superlatives. Deliberate rhythm — sections differ in treatment on purpose; nothing is a uniform reflex.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1, visible focus indicators, semantic landmarks, and labelled form fields with clear error recovery. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` with non-animated fallbacks, and never gate content visibility on scroll-triggered motion. Color is never the sole carrier of meaning.
